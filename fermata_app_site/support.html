Common questions

Where is my data?

On your iPhone, and in your own private iCloud if you turn on sync. I can’t see it. Fermata also keeps about a week of automatic backups on your phone. See the privacy policy for the details.

How do I back up or restore?

In Fermata, open Preferences and choose “Back up now.” If the app’s data is ever unexpectedly empty, it offers to restore from its automatic backups.

Does it work with Google Calendar?

Fermata works with the built-in Calendar app. If your Google account is added in iOS Settings under Calendar, its events appear there and so in Fermata. It doesn’t connect to Google directly.

Why am I not getting reminders?

Check that notifications are allowed for Fermata in iOS Settings, and that the reminder you want is on in Fermata under Preferences, then Reminders. Daily and weekly planning reminders can each be turned on or off and set to any time.

Can I turn the planners off?

Yes. In Preferences, under Planning prompts, switch off the daily planner, the weekly planner, or both. Their offers and reminders then stop.

How do I erase my data?

In Preferences, choose “Erase all data.” This deletes everything in Fermata, including its backups, and it can’t be undone.

Is there an iPad or Android version?

Not at the moment. Fermata is for iPhone, on iOS 17 or later.
