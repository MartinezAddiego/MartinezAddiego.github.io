Fermata privacy policy

Last updated: [date]

Fermata is a planner for seasons, projects, and daily work. This policy explains what it stores, where, and who can see it. In short: your planning data stays with you, and the developer can’t see it.

What Fermata stores

Only what you enter or create in the app:

  • Seasons, projects, milestones, weekly targets, habits, and tasks
  • Calendar blocks, recurring classes and meetings, and days off
  • Focus sessions and your ratings of them, plus the statistics Fermata calculates from them
  • Your settings, such as working hours, working days, and reminder times

Where it’s stored

  • On your device. Fermata keeps your data in its private storage on your iPhone.
  • In your private iCloud account (optional). If you’re signed in to iCloud and sync is on, Fermata uses Apple’s CloudKit to keep your data in your own private iCloud storage so it follows you to a new phone or another device. Only you can access it through your Apple ID. Apple hosts it under its privacy policy and iCloud terms.
  • Automatic backups on your device. Each time you leave the app, Fermata saves a copy of your data in its own storage on your device and keeps about a week of copies. These can be restored if the app’s data is ever unexpectedly empty. They’re included in any iPhone backup you make.

What the developer can access

Nothing. Fermata has no developer server and no account system, and the developer can’t see, collect, or share your planning data.

What Fermata doesn’t do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking
  • No sharing or selling of data, and no third-party services
  • No access to your contacts, photos, or location

Apple may give the developer anonymous, aggregated statistics (such as download counts) and, if you’ve chosen to share diagnostics with app developers in iOS Settings, crash reports. These don’t include your planning data.

Calendars (optional)

If you turn it on, Fermata reads the calendars you choose from the Calendar app on your iPhone to show those events and plan around them, and can add your Fermata blocks to a calendar in the Calendar app on your iPhone that you pick. These are two separate switches, and Fermata does only what you turn on. Events from your calendars are shown live and aren’t copied into Fermata unless you add one yourself. Everything stays on your device; nothing is sent to the developer. You can turn this off at any time in Fermata’s Preferences or in iOS Settings.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Fermata schedules reminders on your device, such as planning reminders and a heads-up before a scheduled block. They aren’t sent through any server, and you choose which ones are on and when they arrive.

Deleting your data

  • In the app, go to Preferences and choose Erase all data. This deletes everything in Fermata, including its automatic backups, on that device and, if iCloud sync is on, on your other devices. It can’t be undone.
  • Deleting the app removes its data and backups from that device. If iCloud sync was on, a copy stays in your iCloud account until you remove it in iOS Settings under your name, then iCloud, then Manage Account Storage.

If you sign out of iCloud

If you sign out of iCloud, or turn iCloud off for Fermata in iOS Settings, iOS removes Fermata’s synced data from that device. It comes back when you sign in again. Fermata’s automatic backups can also be used to restore your data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated, and significant changes will be described in the app’s release notes.

Questions
