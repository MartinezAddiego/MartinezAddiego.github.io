Fermata

A Rhythm for Your Work

One place to plan your seasons, your weeks, and your days, so you can do less and do it better.

Why I built it

I had a lot of planning systems. They worked, but I felt they could be better.

I felt a lot of friction with my planning across multiple time-scales: a notebook for my day-to-day, and Notion for long-term planning and task capture. I kept over- and under-estimating time blocks for tasks, and I would get frustrated trying to put it all together. It was too much. And it was not ideal for me.

I wanted one common location for my productivity-based planning. I built Fermata: A Rhythm for Your Work with one main goal in mind:

Do less, but do it better and more efficiently.

Fermata is not a lifestyle app. It’s a productivity planner. It will help you accomplish your professional, personal, and academic goals, and keep you accountable.

About Fermata

It’s for the student and the professional.

1 · The Command Center

Where your big-scale goals take flight. It all starts at the season level: what are your big projects for this period, and what monthly milestones or deliverables do you need to hit to reach your goals? You choose roughly when to start working toward each milestone, and it floats to your “front burner” when it’s time to pay attention.

2 · The week

What targets do you need to hit this week to reach your milestone? Fermata reminds you to set up your week, which builds the crux of your planning: the daily level.

3 · The day

Time-blocking, built around a few questions: what are your time constraints for this day, and what tasks do you need to complete to reach your target, and how long will they take? If you’d like, Fermata sends a notification in the morning to do it.

What’s inside

Will I make it?

A forecast for the milestones you’re working toward, based on your own numbers.

Guided planning

Short morning and Sunday planners that you can always skip.

Daily habits

Practices measured by pace, not by a deadline.

Your best hours

Learns when you focus best, and helps you protect that time.

About the name

A fermata (fer-MAH-tah) is the mark in music that tells a player to hold a note or a rest longer than written.

It’s a reminder that space is part of the rhythm, and that doing less can be how you do it better.

Why use Fermata?

Get into a rhythm with your work and never look back.

See where your time goes during the day, stay accountable, and make changes in your routine that help you. Fermata is for people struggling to live up to their working potential.

It’s free to use. There’s no account to create, and I never see your data: it’s stored on your phone, and in your own iCloud if you turn on sync.

Fermata is coming soon to iPhone. It works with the built-in Calendar app (calendars added in iOS Settings, including Google, show up there).
